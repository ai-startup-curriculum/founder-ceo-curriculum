# Module 002 — Lean Business Modeling & Unit Economics

> **Stage:** IDEA→PRE-SEED · **Pillar:** Strategy / Economics · **Prereq:** mod-001

**Status:** stub — objectives and target artifact defined; lesson filled by the
autonomous pipeline.

## Objectives

1. Turn discovery findings into a **lean canvas** (problem, segments, value prop,
   channels, revenue, cost structure, key metrics, unfair advantage).
2. Build a first **unit-economics sheet**: what one customer costs to acquire and
   serve, and what they're worth.
3. Identify the **riskiest assumption** in the model and how you'd test it next.

## Founder artifact

A completed lean canvas **and** a unit-economics sheet for your (or the simulated)
business, with the single riskiest assumption flagged.

> Depends on mod-001 because a canvas built without discovery evidence is fiction.
