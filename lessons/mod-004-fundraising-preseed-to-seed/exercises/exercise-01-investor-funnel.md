# Exercise 01 — Investor Funnel from 200 Targets

**Module:** 004 Fundraising: Pre-seed → Seed · **Stage:** PRE-SEED
→SEED · **Time:** ~6–8 focused hours (one working week for an
operating founder) · **Reads with:** chapters
[03](../03-investor-funnel-and-conversion.md) and
[04](../04-parallel-time-boxed-process.md).

## Problem statement

You have a raise sized off your mod-003 operating model (see
exercise 02 in this module) and a rough sense that you should
"pitch some investors." This exercise turns that into a real
**investor funnel** — a segmented target list of ~200 investors,
tier-specific conversion assumptions, an implied term-sheet count
that either supports the round or explains why it doesn't, and a
sequenced batch plan tied to a hard close date.

The exercise is not to produce a "perfect" list. It is to produce
a funnel **specific enough to be tested** — where the number of
implied term sheets falls out of the arithmetic rather than out of
the founder's optimism, and where the plan for running the round
is on the calendar rather than in the wishlist.

## Deliverables (three founder artifacts)

1. **A segmented target list of ~200 investors** — organised into
   tiers by stage fit, sector fit, check-size behaviour, and
   warm-path strength. Each row identifies the fund, the specific
   partner, and how you'd reach them.
2. **A funnel model** — named tier-specific conversion assumptions
   at each stage (target → first meeting → partner meeting → term
   sheet), the arithmetic applied to the segmented list, and the
   implied number of term sheets.
3. **A sequenced batch plan** — the specific 10–20 practice-batch
   investors, the specific A-tier investors opened in parallel,
   the target date for a soft-circled lead, and the hard close
   date for the round.

## Scenario

Pick one:

- **Your real startup** (recommended if you're an operating
  founder): use your **actual** round size (from your mod-003
  model, sized in this module's exercise 02), your real target
  list, and the specific warm intros you can plausibly get this
  quarter.
- **Simulated:** Your mod-003 model implies a **$3M seed** to buy
  18 months of runway and reach the milestone of *"10 paying
  design partners at ≥ $3k ARPA."* Your current network gets you
  strong warm intros to roughly **15 seed funds** and weaker
  paths to roughly **60 more**. The remaining ~125 targets on
  your list will need to be reached via colder channels
  (portfolio-founder cold intros, LinkedIn, conference intros, or
  investor-scout syndicates).

## Requirements

The funnel has to satisfy each of the following, checkably, by the
end of the exercise:

- **Raise summary at the top of the sheet.** Round size, milestone
  the round buys, target close date, months of post-close
  runway. Every downstream number should be consistent with it.
- **~200 named targets.** Each row includes a specific **fund
  name**, **partner name**, stage focus, sector fit (a recent
  adjacent investment counts), typical lead / follow check size,
  named warm-path source, and warm-path score (1–9 per the anchor
  scale in [chapter 03](../03-investor-funnel-and-conversion.md)).
- **Tiered into A / B / C.** A-tier: strong fit + warm path ≥ 6.
  B-tier: good fit, warm path 3–5. C-tier: long-shot fit or cold
  path. A common healthy distribution is ~15–25 A-tier, ~50–80
  B-tier, and the rest C-tier.
- **At least 10 credible lead candidates in the target list.**
  Funds that have led rounds of your size in the last 12 months
  and are a sector fit. A round with < 10 lead candidates has a
  structural problem no follower momentum will fix (see
  [chapter 05](../05-lead-follow-vs-party-round.md)).
- **Tier-specific target → first-meeting conversion rates.** Not
  one flat rate across the whole list. A-tier: 50–70%; B-tier:
  15–25%; C-tier: 1–5%. Downstream stages (first → partner, and
  partner → term sheet) may use the module heuristics unless you
  have defensible tier-specific numbers.
- **Every rate is either cited or explicitly marked ASSUMPTION.**
  A conversion rate you can't source is fine — mark it and use
  it. A conversion rate you pretend is data is not.
- **The arithmetic is computed per tier and summed.** Implied
  term-sheet count falls out of the numbers, not out of the
  founder's read.
- **Sanity checks pass.** Implied term sheets ≥ 2. Implied term
  sheets include ≥ 1 from a fund on your credible-lead list. If
  either fails, the funnel needs to change before the round
  opens.
- **A batch plan on a calendar.** Practice batch (10–20 low-cost
  investors) named and dated; A-tier open-in-parallel week
  named; target soft-circled-lead week named; target close week
  named.

## Starter guidance

**Session 1 (~1 hr) — the raise summary.** Read
[chapter 02](../02-raise-amount-milestone-dilution.md) if you
haven't already, or the summary at the top of your sheet from
exercise 02 in this module. Write the raise summary at the top of
this exercise's sheet. Every subsequent number is consistent with
this one.

**Session 2 (~3–4 hr) — the target list.** Read
[chapter 03](../03-investor-funnel-and-conversion.md). Open a
spreadsheet with columns: fund, partner, stage focus, sector fit
(recent adjacent investment: yes/no + example), typical check as
lead ($), typical check as follow ($), warm-path source (named
person), warm-path score (1–9), tier. Populate as many rows as you
can with real names. Sources to lean on: fund websites' recent
investments pages, LinkedIn 2nd-degree connections mapped against
partner names, mutual-fund-portfolio founders who might intro,
Crunchbase / PitchBook portfolio pages, sector-specific investor
lists from sources like Signal (NFX), OpenVC, or Visible.

If you can't reach 200 rows with real names, that is a finding —
the network gap is the first problem to fix, not the funnel.

**Session 3 (~1 hr) — segment and count.** Bucket each row into A
/ B / C tier per the anchor scale in
[chapter 03](../03-investor-funnel-and-conversion.md). Count each
tier. Count credible lead candidates. If A-tier is under 10 or
lead-capable count is under 10, stop and spend a week on
warm-path building before the round opens.

**Session 4 (~1 hr) — funnel arithmetic.** Set tier-specific
target → first-meeting rates; set the downstream rates. Compute
per-tier term-sheet counts and sum. Run the sanity checks
(≥ 2 sheets, ≥ 1 lead among them). If either fails, name the
cheapest fix — grow tier A (usually), grow the list (less
leverage), or shrink the round (last resort).

**Session 5 (~1 hr) — batch plan on the calendar.** Read
[chapter 04](../04-parallel-time-boxed-process.md). Pick 10–20
practice-batch targets from B or C tier. Set the A-tier
open-in-parallel week. Set the target soft-circled-lead week and
the target close week. Put dates on all four, not "week X of the
round."

## Acceptance criteria

You are done — and this exercise has met the module's standard —
when *all* of the following are true:

- [ ] The raise summary is stated at the top and every downstream
      number is consistent with it.
- [ ] ~200 rows are populated with named funds, named partners,
      and named warm-path sources (or explicit "cold").
- [ ] Rows are bucketed into A / B / C tiers per the anchor scale.
- [ ] Tier counts include **≥ 10 credible lead candidates**.
- [ ] Tier-specific target → first-meeting rates are named and
      either cited or marked ASSUMPTION.
- [ ] Per-tier and total implied term-sheet counts are computed.
- [ ] Both sanity checks pass, or a cheapest fix is named.
- [ ] The practice-batch, A-tier open-in-parallel, soft-circled-
      lead, and close dates are on the calendar.

## Self-assessment rubric

| Dimension | Weak | Strong |
|---|---|---|
| Raise anchor | Amount picked from vibes | Raise size and milestone from mod-003 model at the top of the sheet |
| Target list | "VCs" as a category | ~200 named funds + named partners, sourced from portfolio research |
| Segmentation | One flat list | Tiered by stage fit, sector fit, check behaviour, warm-path score |
| Lead coverage | No lead candidates named | ≥ 10 credible lead candidates identified in the target list |
| Warm-path realism | Every intro scored an 8 | Honest scoring with named intro sources; A-tier count is defensible |
| Conversion assumptions | One rate for the whole funnel | Tier-specific rates, each cited or marked ASSUMPTION |
| Implied outcomes | "We'll get some meetings" | Term-sheet count computed; ≥ 2 sheets and ≥ 1 lead required to proceed |
| Batching / timing | "We'll pitch everyone" | Practice batch, parallel A-tier open, dated close, soft-lead target |

## Definition of done

A skeptical seed-stage investor or a founder who has run a raise
recently would read your funnel and agree that (a) the target
list is real, not aspirational, (b) the conversion assumptions
are defensible for your warm-path strength and sector, (c) the
funnel implies enough term sheets — including at least one
credible lead — to justify opening the round, and (d) the batch
plan is on the calendar, not the wishlist.
