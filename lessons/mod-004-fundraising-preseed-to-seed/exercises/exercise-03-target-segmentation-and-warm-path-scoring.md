# Exercise 03 — Target Segmentation & Warm-Path Scoring

**Module:** 004 Fundraising: Pre-seed → Seed · **Stage:** PRE-SEED
→SEED · **Time:** ~2 focused hours · **Reads with:** chapters
[03](../03-investor-funnel-and-conversion.md) and
[05](../05-lead-follow-vs-party-round.md).

## Problem statement

Exercise 01 asked you to *build* a 200-target funnel. This
exercise is a smaller, focused drill on the two disciplines that
most often break that funnel in practice: **honest tier
segmentation** and **honest warm-path scoring**. Do this before
you open the round; do it as a standalone diagnostic if you're
mid-round and the funnel isn't producing meetings.

The specific failure this exercise catches: tier-A count that
looks defensible on the sheet but collapses under scrutiny
because every warm-path score is a 7, every "sector fit" is a
generous read, and every "lead-capable" flag is aspirational.

## Deliverables (three founder artifacts)

1. **A hardened tier-A list of 15–25 investors** — each row
   passes a specific evidence check on stage, sector, lead
   capability, and warm path. No score is unjustified.
2. **A separate credible-lead sublist** — the funds in your
   target list that have led rounds of your size in the last 12
   months and are a sector fit. Must contain ≥ 10 names.
3. **A warm-path gap plan** — for any A-tier slot that fails the
   evidence check, the specific action (intro to ask for, event
   to attend, portfolio founder to reach) that would upgrade it,
   with a date.

## Scenario

Pick one:

- **Your real startup** (recommended): use the target list from
  exercise 01 or your existing pipeline sheet. This exercise
  works best on a real list; the drill is precisely to be honest
  about which rows are real.
- **Simulated:** You have a 200-row target list from exercise 01
  where you've assigned tiers. Assume you've been generous —
  many warm-path scores are inflated, several "sector fit"
  entries are aspirational rather than evidence-backed, and the
  "lead-capable" column is largely unfilled.

## The evidence checks

For each candidate A-tier row, answer each of the following with a
**yes** and a piece of evidence, or the row does not belong in
A-tier.

### Stage focus check
- Does this fund's **recent activity** (last 12 months, not
  "about us" page) include rounds at your stage?
- **Evidence:** at least one named recent deal at your stage from
  this fund.

### Sector fit check
- Does this fund have a **recent (< 18 months) adjacent
  investment** or an actively-stated thesis that names your
  space?
- **Evidence:** name the portfolio company or the specific
  thesis writing (podcast, blog, LinkedIn essay).

### Lead capability check (for lead candidates)
- Has this fund **led a round of your size** in the last 12
  months?
- **Evidence:** the announcement language — "led by \[fund\]" —
  from a recent public deal at your check size.

### Warm-path evidence check
- Is your warm-path source a **specific named person** who has
  agreed (implicitly or explicitly) to make the intro?
- **Evidence:** either a prior conversation with the intro-maker
  in which the intro was discussed, or an existing relationship
  strong enough that the ask is a Slack message, not a
  cold-outreach coffee.

The scoring anchor:

- **Warm-path 9** — founder-they-backed who exited well and has
  offered the intro.
- **Warm-path 7** — current portfolio-company founder or a peer
  operator they respect, who has met you and would introduce
  with a real endorsement.
- **Warm-path 5** — mutual acquaintance who has worked with them
  and would send a considered intro.
- **Warm-path 3** — mutual acquaintance who "could probably
  intro" without strong prior context.
- **Warm-path 1** — cold LinkedIn, cold email, cold conference
  intro.

**If you cannot name the specific intro-maker, the score is a 1.**
Not a 4 "because I know someone who might know them." A 1.

## Requirements

- **Run all four evidence checks on every A-tier candidate.** A
  row that passes stage + sector + warm-path is A-tier; a row
  that fails any is B-tier or drops out.
- **Build the credible-lead sublist.** Separate from tiering:
  filter the whole target list by the lead-capability check.
  Must be ≥ 10 names. If it isn't, the round has a structural
  lead-coverage problem to solve before opening.
- **Recount the tiers.** After the evidence checks, count A / B /
  C again. Compare to your exercise 01 counts. If A-tier
  contracted meaningfully (30%+), name why — this is the honesty
  moment.
- **Warm-path gap plan.** For each row that was A-tier under
  generous scoring but failed the warm-path evidence check, name
  the specific upgrade action (intro to ask for, portfolio
  founder to reach, event to attend) and the week you'll do it.
- **Recompute the funnel arithmetic.** Using the *hardened* tier
  counts, re-run the per-tier arithmetic from exercise 01. Does
  the implied term-sheet count still support the round?

## Starter guidance

**Session 1 (~45 min) — the evidence pass.** For each candidate
A-tier row, do a 5-minute check:

- Open the fund's recent-investments page (or Crunchbase /
  PitchBook if you have access). Confirm stage.
- Search for "\[partner name\] \[your sector keyword\]" on
  Google, Twitter/X, and podcasts. Confirm sector fit.
- Read one recent public deal announcement from the fund.
  Confirm lead capability if you're marking them as a lead
  candidate.
- Open Slack / email / LinkedIn. Confirm the warm-path source is
  a specific person you can actually contact this week.

Downgrade any row that fails any check.

**Session 2 (~30 min) — the credible-lead sublist.** Filter the
whole list by lead capability. Count. If < 10, escalate to the
warm-path gap plan.

**Session 3 (~30 min) — the gap plan.** For each downgraded row
and each missing lead-capable slot, write one line:

```
[Fund] — need: [what would upgrade this] — action: [specific ask]
        — who: [name] — by: [date]
```

**Session 4 (~15 min) — re-run the funnel arithmetic.** Using
the hardened tier counts, apply the tier-specific target →
first-meeting rates from
[chapter 03](../03-investor-funnel-and-conversion.md). Recompute
the implied term-sheet count. Does the honest funnel still
support the round?

## Acceptance criteria

You are done when *all* of the following are true:

- [ ] Every A-tier row has passed all four evidence checks (stage,
      sector, lead capability if applicable, warm path with a
      named person).
- [ ] The credible-lead sublist has **≥ 10 names**; if not, the
      warm-path gap plan explicitly addresses the gap.
- [ ] Tier counts are recorded before and after the evidence
      pass. Any large A-tier contraction is named honestly.
- [ ] Every downgrade has a warm-path gap plan line: **specific
      action, specific person, specific date**.
- [ ] The honest funnel arithmetic has been re-run and the go /
      no-go decision on opening the round is stated.

## Self-assessment rubric

| Dimension | Weak | Strong |
|---|---|---|
| Evidence checks | Applied to none / a few rows | Applied to every A-tier candidate, with evidence recorded |
| Warm-path honesty | "Someone will intro me" | Named person per row; no named person → score of 1 |
| Lead coverage | Not tracked | Separate credible-lead sublist with ≥ 10 names |
| Gap plan | Missing rows just disappear | Every downgraded row has an upgrade action + date |
| Funnel honesty | Re-run against original counts | Re-run against hardened counts; go / no-go stated |

## Definition of done

A skeptical peer founder — one who has run a raise recently and
knows the tell for inflated scoring — would read your hardened
A-tier and agree that (a) every row passes the evidence checks,
(b) the credible-lead sublist is defensible, and (c) the warm-
path gap plan is a set of asks you could actually execute this
week.
