# Module 005 — Equity, SAFEs & Cap Tables

> **Stage:** PRE-SEED→SEED · **Pillar:** Equity · **Prereq:** mod-004

**Status:** stub — objectives and target artifact defined; lesson filled by the
autonomous pipeline.

## Objectives

1. Read a **cap table** and compute ownership through rounds.
2. Understand **SAFEs** (cap, discount, post- vs pre-money) and how they convert.
3. Size an **option pool** and understand dilution — who pays for it and when.

## Founder artifact — the exercise

> Model the cap table for: **founder + cofounder**, a **10% option pool**, a **SAFE**
> (say $500k at a $5M post-money cap), and a **priced seed** ($2M at $8M pre).
> Calculate everyone's ownership at each step and after the priced round converts
> the SAFE. Show where the option pool dilution lands.

Deliverable: the cap table (table/spreadsheet) with per-step ownership %.

> Depends on mod-004: instruments (SAFEs, priced rounds) come from the raise you
> planned there.
