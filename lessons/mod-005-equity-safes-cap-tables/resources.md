  [chapter 03](./03-safe-anatomy.md) and the conversion math in
  [chapter 04](./04-post-money-safe-conversion-math.md).

## Tax, securities, and plan-administration references

- **Internal Revenue Code §409A and Treasury Regulations
  thereunder** (IRS). The statutory basis for the 409A valuation
  requirement — the FMV determination that sets the strike price
  of employee options. Referenced in
  [chapter 02](./02-four-share-classes.md) and the boundary note
  in [chapter 10](./10-boundaries-finance-and-cap-table-craft.md).
  [irs.gov](https://www.irs.gov/).
  <!-- needs-research: verify stable IRS.gov URLs for §409A guidance; Treasury Regulations §1.409A-1 through §1.409A-6 are the operative sections. -->
- **Internal Revenue Code §422 (Incentive Stock Options) and §83
  (property transferred in connection with the performance of
  services), including §83(b) elections** (IRS). Statutory
  anchors for ISO vs NSO tax treatment and the 83(b) election
  mechanics referenced in
  [chapter 02](./02-four-share-classes.md).
  <!-- needs-research: verify stable IRS.gov URLs for §422, §83, and §83(b); IRS Publication 525 (Taxable and Nontaxable Income) is the practitioner-facing anchor. -->
- **Internal Revenue Code §1202 (Qualified Small Business Stock).**
  Governs the QSBS exclusion from capital gains tax on
  qualifying founder and early-investor stock. Referenced by the
  boundary note in
  [chapter 10](./10-boundaries-finance-and-cap-table-craft.md).
  IRS Publication 550 is the practitioner-facing anchor.
  <!-- needs-research: verify stable IRS.gov URLs for §1202 and Publication 550; QSBS rules were materially expanded by the One Big Beautiful Bill Act in 2025 — verify the current eligibility thresholds, holding period, and exclusion amount against enacted law. -->
- **Securities Act of 1933 Regulation D, Rules 506(b) and 506(c)**
  (SEC). The private-placement safe harbors that most seed-stage
  SAFE and priced rounds rely on; counsel will cite them in the
  closing checklist referenced in
  [chapter 09](./09-briefing-counsel-and-the-redline.md).
  [sec.gov](https://www.sec.gov/).
  <!-- needs-research: verify stable SEC.gov URLs for Rule 506(b) and Rule 506(c); the SEC Small Business compliance guide and 17 CFR §230.506 are the operative anchors. -->
- **Securities Exchange Act Rule 13e-4 (SEC)** and related staff
  guidance on tender offers. The compliance anchor for secondary
  tender offers to employees, referenced by the boundary note in
  [chapter 10](./10-boundaries-finance-and-cap-table-craft.md).
  <!-- needs-research: verify stable SEC.gov URL for Rule 13e-4 and current staff interpretations on employee tender offers. -->
- **National Center for Employee Ownership (NCEO).**
  [nceo.org](https://www.nceo.org/). Non-profit reference on
  employee-stock-plan administration, option-grant mechanics,
  and plan-design trade-offs beyond the seed stage. Referenced

## Notes on citation hygiene

Where a specific claim in this module cites a fact that would
benefit from a primary source and I could not verify one at
authoring time, the chapter has an inline `<!-- needs-research:
... -->` comment marking the gap. These do not block reading;
they mark items for later verification and block the automated
merge pipeline until resolved. If you spot a citation that
should be present or a source that has been superseded, opening
an issue against this repository is the fastest way to fix it.

Round sizes, option-pool bands, grant ranges, and dilution
numbers in this module reflect roughly the last several US-
market cycles. They move with the venture cycle and vary by
geography and sector; treat the ranges as the *shape* of the
decision, not as lookup values. The Carta and PitchBook-NVCA
data references above are the empirical anchors to re-check
against the current cycle.

Tax, securities, and plan-administration references above are
provided as pointers for founder reading and counsel briefing.
They are **not** legal or tax advice; retain qualified counsel
and a tax advisor for any decision that depends on the
application of these rules to your specific facts (see the
counsel-handoff discipline in
[chapter 09](./09-briefing-counsel-and-the-redline.md)).
