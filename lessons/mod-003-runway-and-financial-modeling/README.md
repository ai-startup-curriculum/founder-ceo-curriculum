# Module 003 — Runway & Financial Modeling

> **Stage:** SEED · **Pillar:** Finance · **Prereq:** mod-002

**Status:** stub — objectives and target artifact defined; lesson filled by the
autonomous pipeline.

## Objectives

1. Read and build the three founder numbers: **cash, burn, runway**.
2. Build an **operating model** that ties hiring and spend to runway.
3. Reason about **default-alive vs default-dead** and what a raise buys you.

## Founder artifact — the exercise

> You have **$310k in the bank**, are burning **$42k/month**, and plan to **hire two
> engineers**. Build the **18-month operating plan**: month-by-month cash, the new
> burn after hiring, the month you hit zero, and the raise/cut decision points.

Deliverable: a runway model (spreadsheet or table) + a one-paragraph narrative of
what the model tells you to do.
