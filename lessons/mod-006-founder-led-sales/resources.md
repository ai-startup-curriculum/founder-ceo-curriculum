# Resources — Module 006 Founder-led Sales v0

Real, citable references for the material in this module. Primary
sources are listed first per topic; secondary sources for narrower
application (worked examples, community write-ups) follow.

## Books (primary references)

- **Rob Fitzpatrick, *The Mom Test: How to Talk to Customers &
  Learn if Your Business is a Good Idea When Everyone is Lying
  to You* (self-published, 2013).** The canonical discovery-
  interview method used across mod-001 and extended into sales
  contexts in [chapter 02](./02-discovery-in-a-sales-context.md).
  Author's site: [momtestbook.com](https://momtestbook.com/).
  <!-- needs-research: ISBN / stable identifier for The Mom Test, 2013 edition. -->
- **Andy Whyte, *MEDDICC: The ultimate guide to staying one step
  ahead in the complex sale* (Force Management / self-published,
  2020).** The most widely cited modern book-length treatment of
  MEDDIC / MEDDPICC — the qualification framework in
  [chapter 03](./03-hard-qualification-and-disqualification.md).
  Whyte's site: [meddicc.com](https://www.meddicc.com/).
  <!-- needs-research: verify publication year and publisher; MEDDICC has no single canonical primary source and is commonly attributed to Jack Napoli et al. at PTC in the 1990s. -->
- **Aaron Ross & Marylou Tyler, *Predictable Revenue: Turn Your
  Business Into a Sales Machine with the $100 Million Best
  Practices of Salesforce.com* (PebbleStorm, 2011).** The
  originating text for the SDR/AE-split model and the outbound
  playbook that most modern early-stage SaaS sales organizations
  build on. Referenced from the transition-signal discussion in
  [chapter 07](./07-transition-signal-to-first-sales-hire.md).
- **Mark Roberge, *The Sales Acceleration Formula: Using Data,
  Technology, and Inbound Selling to go from $0 to $100 Million*
  (Wiley, 2015).** HubSpot's former CRO on scaling from founder-
  led sales to a first sales team; the transition-signal chapter
  draws on the same "hire on the playbook, not on the hope"
  discipline.
- **Aaron Ross & Jason Lemkin, *From Impossible to Inevitable:
  How Hyper-Growth Companies Create Predictable Revenue* (Wiley,
  2016).** SaaStr's Lemkin plus Ross on the seven ingredients of
  hyper-growth; particularly useful on the pattern of founder-
  led-motion → hire-first-AE → build-team.
- **Chris Voss, *Never Split the Difference: Negotiating As If
  Your Life Depended On It* (Harper Business, 2016).** Not a
  sales book per se, but the discovery-first negotiation posture
  ("calibrated questions," "no-oriented questions") is closely
  aligned with the Mom Test posture in
  [chapter 02](./02-discovery-in-a-sales-context.md) and the
  contract-negotiation posture in
  [chapter 05](./05-pilot-to-enterprise-contract.md).

## Sales-methodology references

- **MEDDIC / MEDDPICC** — commonly attributed to Jack Napoli and
  colleagues at PTC in the 1990s; formalized and popularized by
  Force Management and MEDDIC Academy. No single canonical
  primary source; Andy Whyte's *MEDDICC* (above) is the standard
  book reference. See also
  [force-management.com/meddicc](https://www.force-management.com/meddicc)
  and [meddic.academy](https://meddic.academy/).
  <!-- needs-research: verify stable URLs; MEDDIC branding has migrated between organizations over time. -->
- **BANT** — Budget, Authority, Need, Timeline; commonly
  attributed to IBM sales training in the 1960s. No single
  canonical primary source. Secondary treatments include
  HubSpot's *Sales Blog*
  ([blog.hubspot.com/sales](https://blog.hubspot.com/sales)) and
  the Sales Hacker community (see below).
- **CHAMP** (Challenges, Authority, Money, Prioritization) and
  **GPCTBA/C&I** (HubSpot's Goals, Plans, Challenges, Timeline,
  Budget, Authority, Consequences, Implications) — modern
  alternatives to BANT surfacing in
  [chapter 03](./03-hard-qualification-and-disqualification.md).
  Both are HubSpot-adjacent frameworks; primary references on
  the HubSpot blog.
- **Winning by Design content library**
  ([winningbydesign.com/library](https://winningbydesign.com/library)).
  Long-running practitioner writing on B2B sales methodology,
  the SaaS bowtie funnel, and the SPICED discovery framework —
  a modern successor to MEDDIC-family qualification.

  interviews with founders and sales leaders on the founder-led
  motion and its transition to a scaled team.

## Contract-paper templates (primary references)

- **Bonterms — free, open-source model contracts**
  ([bonterms.com/forms](https://bonterms.com/forms/)). A
  consortium of general counsel from tech companies (including
  Cloudflare, HubSpot, Twilio) publishes standard-form MSA,
  DPA, MNDA, and other contract templates under a Creative
  Commons license. The best starting point for a founder who
  needs a template MSA before commissioning one from counsel.
  <!-- needs-research: verify current membership and template library at bonterms.com; the consortium has grown over time. -->
- **Common Paper**
  ([commonpaper.com](https://commonpaper.com/)). Modern cloud-
  service agreement templates and a standardized DPA; a
  narrower alternative to Bonterms with a focus on SaaS
  agreements.
- **NVCA Model Legal Documents**
  ([nvca.org/model-legal-documents](https://nvca.org/model-legal-documents/)).
  Primarily investor-side documents (Series Seed / Series A),
  but the associated indemnification, IP assignment, and
  employment agreement templates are useful references for
  sales-adjacent employment paper.
- **Y Combinator, *SAFE and other documents*
  ([ycombinator.com/documents](https://www.ycombinator.com/documents)).**
  Not a sales contract library, but the same "founder-first,
  legally-defensible, standardized" philosophy applies to the
  paper approach in this module.

## Security and privacy compliance references

- **AICPA SOC 2 Trust Services Criteria.**
  [aicpa-cima.com/topic/audit-assurance/audit-and-assurance-greater-than-soc-2](https://www.aicpa-cima.com/topic/audit-assurance/audit-and-assurance-greater-than-soc-2).
  The primary reference for SOC 2 Type II audits — the most
  common enterprise-buyer security ask.
- **Cloud Security Alliance CAIQ (Consensus Assessments
  Initiative Questionnaire).**
  [cloudsecurityalliance.org/research/cloud-controls-matrix](https://cloudsecurityalliance.org/research/cloud-controls-matrix).
  The standard security questionnaire many enterprise buyers
  send.
- **Shared Assessments SIG (Standardized Information Gathering)
  questionnaire.** [sharedassessments.org/sig](https://sharedassessments.org/sig/).
  A broader third-party risk-assessment questionnaire used by
  larger enterprise buyers.
- **GDPR — Regulation (EU) 2016/679.** Full text at
  [eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2016/679/oj](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2016/679/oj).
  Article 28 governs processor obligations (the basis for the
  DPA in [chapter 05](./05-pilot-to-enterprise-contract.md));
  Article 35 governs Data Protection Impact Assessments.
- **HIPAA — 45 CFR §160 through §164.** The Office for Civil
  Rights (OCR) at HHS maintains the primary guidance at
  [hhs.gov/hipaa/for-professionals](https://www.hhs.gov/hipaa/for-professionals/index.html).
  §164.502(e) and §164.504(e) cover the Business Associate
  Agreement requirements.
- **Schrems II — CJEU Case C-311/18 (July 2020)** and the
  subsequent **European Commission Standard Contractual
  Clauses, Implementing Decision (EU) 2021/914.** Primary
  documents at
  [eur-lex.europa.eu](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/). Governs
  cross-border data transfer post-Privacy Shield.
- **Vanta, Drata, Secureframe** — the three market-leading
  automated-compliance platforms for startup SOC 2, ISO 27001,
  and HIPAA readiness. Practitioner guides on their sites are
  useful for the operational side of the compliance path.
  [vanta.com](https://www.vanta.com/), [drata.com](https://drata.com/),
  [secureframe.com](https://secureframe.com/).

## Adjacent-track references (per boundaries in chapter 08)

- **`startup-product-gtm-curriculum`** (level 30) — the peer
  track that owns post-founder-led GTM: first SDR/AE hire and
  ramp, quota-carrying rep management, marketing engine, formal
  sales org, channel strategy, customer success as a function,
  and pricing/packaging as a permanent GTM discipline.
- **`cpo-curriculum`** — the pathway that owns continuous
  discovery and opportunity-solution trees; the discovery
  learning surfaced during founder-led sales feeds this
  discipline.
- **`startup-operations-governance-curriculum`** — the pathway
  that owns board-level compensation-committee mechanics, plus
  the governance framing for major sales-adjacent decisions
  (e.g., pricing changes that require board consent under some
  investor rights agreements).
- **`startup-foundations`** — company formation, stage
  vocabulary, and the pillar map that situates GTM inside the
  wider startup curriculum.
- **`cto-curriculum`** — how the CTO supports pilots (technical
  scoping, integration work, security-questionnaire responses)
  during the founder-led motion.
