# Module 006 — Founder-led Sales v0

> **Stage:** SEED · **Pillar:** GTM / Sales · **Prereq:** mod-002

Nobody knows who you are. There is no brand, no category, no
inbound, no reference customer. In that environment, **the
founder is the sales channel** — not because founders are
naturally good at sales, but because the two things early deals
need are things nobody else can supply: discovery-grade
curiosity, and real authority to bend the deal in the room.

The founder-led sales motion is discovery → qualification →
pilot → contract, run personally by the founder, every step,
until the playbook is repeatable enough to hand to a first
sales hire. Delegating any step too early doesn't buy the
founder time back — it costs the company the learning that
step was supposed to produce, and the roadmap and the pricing
built on top of that learning drift out of contact with the
market.

The single most expensive mistake in this module is treating
the pilot as a free trial. A pilot without a written success
metric, a fixed end date, and a signed agreement is a free
consulting engagement the founder cannot exit. The chapters
that follow build the discipline that keeps pilots from
becoming consulting: a pre-agreed quantitative metric owned
by the buyer's side, a 4–8 week duration, and a written path
from a hit metric to a signed contract.

Deeper post-founder-led GTM craft — first SDR/AE hire, quota-
carrying reps, marketing engine, formal sales org, channel
strategy — is deferred to the `startup-product-gtm-curriculum`
peer track (level 30). Contract paper drafting — MSA, DPA,
BAA — is routed to legal counsel. Both handoffs are made
explicit in [chapter 08](./08-boundaries-gtm-track-and-counsel.md).

## Learning objectives

After this module you can:

1. **Run the founder-led sales motion** when nobody knows who
   you are — discovery → qualification → pilot → contract,
   with the founder doing every step personally before hiring
   sales.
2. **Design a discovery call in a sales context** that
   continues the mod-001 Mom Test method — ask about the last
   time the pain happened, what they tried, what it cost —
   rather than pitching the product.
3. **Qualify hard**: define who is *not* a fit and disqualify
   them explicitly, using MEDDPICC (or BANT+C) and segment-fit
   signals from mod-002 rather than optimism.
4. **Design a pilot** with a scoped success metric, a fixed
   duration (4–8 weeks), and a written contract — knowing
   that a pilot without a success metric is a free consulting
   engagement.
5. **Path a successful pilot to an enterprise contract**:
   procurement, security review, DPA / BAA, master service
   agreement, and the first annual contract value.
6. **Extract the learning** that only shows up when the
   founder sells personally — the actual objections, the
   actual buyer, the actual price the market will pay — and
   feed it back into mod-001 discovery and mod-002 pricing.
7. **Recognise the transition signal**: when the founder-led
   motion has produced ~$500k–$2M ARR across ~5–15 named
   customers with a repeatable playbook, it is time to hire
   the first SDR / AE — this module ends there.
8. **Cite the boundary to `startup-product-gtm-curriculum`**
   (level 30) for post-founder-led GTM.
9. **Cite the boundary to legal counsel** for MSA / DPA /
   BAA / procurement-paper authoring — the CEO negotiates and
   signs, counsel drafts and reviews.

## Exercises

The exercises progress from writing a full sales script for one
real prospect, to a focused qualification drill, to a pilot-
authoring drill. Do them in order for the first pass; on live-
lab reruns, order to match whichever deal you're actually
working.

| # | Exercise | Time | Reads with chapter |
|---|---|---|---|
| 01 | [Sales Script & Pilot Design](./exercises/exercise-01-sales-script-and-pilot.md) | ~6–8 hr | 01–05 |
| 02 | [Hard Qualification Drill](./exercises/exercise-02-hard-qualification-drill.md) | ~2 hr | 03 |
| 03 | [Pilot Scope & Success Metric Authoring](./exercises/exercise-03-pilot-scope-and-success-metric-authoring.md) | ~2–3 hr | 04 |

Exercise 01 is the load-bearing walk-through that most
references the rest of the module. Exercise 02 is a focused
drill on MEDDPICC-and-disqualification you can rerun deal-by-
deal. Exercise 03 is a metric-authoring drill you can rerun
every time you design a new pilot.

Solutions are not provided in this repository; they live in

## Live-lab note

If you're an operating founder using this as a live lab: run
[exercise 01](./exercises/exercise-01-sales-script-and-pilot.md)
against **one named prospect** on your current pipeline this
week. Write the discovery script, qualify hard, design the
pilot, and rehearse the call before you take it. Then take
the call, capture verbatim buyer language, and let it rewrite
your mod-002 canvas and your qualification rubric.

The transition signal in [chapter 07](./07-transition-signal-to-first-sales-hire.md)
is the exit criterion for this module. Below that signal, the
material here is the current work. Above it, the material
remains true but the operating pattern is different — and the
next steps are in the `startup-product-gtm-curriculum` peer
track.
