# Module 001 — Customer Discovery & Idea Validation

> **Stage:** IDEA → PRE-SEED · **Pillar:** Product / Discovery · **Prereqs:** none
> (start here after `startup-foundations`)

Every downstream module — modeling, fundraising, sales — is a bet on a
belief about a customer. Customer discovery is where you find out whether
that belief survives contact with reality *before* you spend 18 months and
other people's money on it. This module owns the **first discovery
sprint**: framing a testable problem hypothesis, running Mom-Test-clean
interviews inside a narrow segment, synthesising a defensible insight
memo, and committing to a written go / pivot / kill call. Continuous
discovery as a permanent product-function discipline is deferred to the
CPO track (see [chapter 07](./07-boundary-cpo-continuous-discovery.md)).

## Learning objectives

After this module you can:

1. Distinguish a **problem hypothesis** from a **solution hypothesis**,
   and know which one discovery tests first.
2. Recruit and run **Mom-Test-clean customer interviews** that surface
   behavior and evidence rather than politeness and speculation —
   past-tense, non-leading, behavioral.
3. Recognise the **four signals a problem is real** — prior spend, a
   specific recent story, a quantified cost, and an existing hacky
   workaround.
4. **Size and segment** the discovery target narrowly enough to produce
   signal (~15–20 interviews in a recognisable segment, not "founders"
   or "SMBs").
5. Synthesise a set of interviews into an **insight memo** that a
   skeptical co-founder or investor would find honest — counted
   patterns rather than a favourite quote.
6. Make a defensible **go / pivot / kill call** and name the
   disconfirming evidence that would change it.
7. Locate the **boundary to `cpo-curriculum`** — this module owns the
   first sprint; the CPO track owns continuous discovery as a permanent
   function.

## Exercises

The exercises are designed as a progression: narrow the segment, author
the guide, then run the sprint that puts both into contact with real
buyers.

| # | Exercise | Time | Reads with chapter |
|---|---|---|---|
| 03 | [Segment-Narrowing Drill](./exercises/exercise-03-segment-narrowing-drill.md) | ~90 min | 04 |
| 02 | [Mom-Test Interview Guide Authoring](./exercises/exercise-02-mom-test-interview-guide-authoring.md) | ~2 hr | 02 |
| 01 | [Customer Discovery Sprint](./exercises/exercise-01-discovery-sprint.md) | ~1 week (6–8 focused hr) | all |

Do exercise 03 first (segment), exercise 02 second (guide), exercise 01
last (the sprint that uses both). The exercise files are numbered by
canonical order in the module, not by the order to attempt them.

Solutions are not provided in this repository; they live in the paired
solutions repo. Worked reference deliverables — showing what "strong"
looks like on exercise 01's two artifacts — are in
[`exemplars/mod-001-customer-discovery/`](../../exemplars/mod-001-customer-discovery/).
