# Exercise 01 — Customer Discovery Sprint

**Module:** 001 Customer Discovery · **Stage:** IDEA→PRE-SEED · **Time:** ~1 week
(≈6–8 focused hours)

## Deliverables (two founder artifacts)

1. **A discovery plan** — a segment definition, a recruit list of 15–20 targets, and
   an interview guide (5–8 open, past-tense, non-leading questions).
2. **An insight memo** — a synthesis across your interviews ending in an
   evidence-backed **go / pivot / kill** call.

## Scenario

Pick one:

- **Your real startup** (recommended if you're an operating founder): take your
  single riskiest *problem* hypothesis and run this on it this week.
- **Simulated:** you believe *"seed-stage technical founders are overwhelmed by
  simultaneous, unordered exposure to fundraising, corporate law, equity, and
  GTM, and would value a staged, dependency-ordered curriculum."* Test the
  **problem** (overwhelm / lack of a map), not the solution (a curriculum).

## Steps

1. **Define the segment** narrowly. Write one sentence: who exactly, and how you'd
   recognize one. Reject "founders"; accept "solo technical founders who
   incorporated and started raising a pre-seed in the last 12 months."
2. **Write the interview guide.** 5–8 questions. Every question must pass the Mom
   Test: about their life and past behavior, never your idea, never hypothetical.
   Include at least one "tell me about the last time…" and one "what have you
   already tried / spent?"
3. **Build a recruit list** of 15–20 names/sources (your network, communities,
   cold outreach, warm intros). Note how you'll reach each.
4. **Run the interviews** (or, for the simulated path, run at least 5 realistic
   role-played interviews and note where you'd expand). Capture notes per the
   template below immediately after each.
5. **Synthesize** into the insight memo. Count how many independently surfaced the
   pain unprompted; list what they've already tried; quantify the cost; note
   surprises and disconfirming evidence.
6. **Decide:** go / pivot / kill, and state the *evidence that would change your
   mind*.

## Per-interview capture template

```
Person / segment fit:
Specific recent story (with detail):
What they've already tried / spent (time or money):
Quantified pain (if any):
Surprises / disconfirming evidence:
```

## Rubric (self-assess, then compare to the exemplar)

| Dimension | Weak | Strong |
|---|---|---|
| Segment | "Founders" | Narrow, recognizable, one sentence |
| Questions | Leading / hypothetical ("Would you use…?") | Past-tense, behavioral, Mom-Test clean |
| Evidence | Opinions & compliments | Stories, prior spend, quantified cost |
| Synthesis | Cherry-picked favorite quote | Counted patterns across the set |
| Decision | "Everyone loved it, go!" | Honest call + disconfirming evidence named |

## Definition of done

A skeptical co-founder reading your insight memo would agree your go/pivot/kill
call is supported by the evidence you gathered — or could point to exactly which
evidence is missing.
