# Discovery Plan (Artifact #1) — Worked Exemplar

## Segment (one sentence)

Solo or two-person **technical founders who incorporated and began raising a
pre-seed or seed round in the last 12 months** — recognizable by a live cap table,
a first fundraising conversation on the calendar, and no prior operating-CEO
experience.

Why this narrow: they're mid-firehose (law + equity + fundraising + GTM arriving
at once) and recent enough to remember the confusion precisely.

## Recruit strategy (target 15–20)

| Channel | Target count | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Personal founder network | 5 | Warmest; ask for the specific segment, not "founders" |
| Accelerator / cohort Slack & Discord | 6 | Post asking for 20-min calls about "the first 6 months" |
| Warm intros from investors/operators | 4 | "Who did you back in the last year that's technical?" |
| Cold outreach (LinkedIn, recent raise announcements) | 5 | Reference their raise; ask about *before* it |

## Interview guide (Mom-Test clean)

Open with: *"I'm trying to understand what the first few months of running a
company are actually like — I'm not selling anything. Can you walk me through
yours?"*

1. Walk me through the last month of running the company — what actually took your
   time? *(life, not idea)*
2. Tell me about the last time you had to make a decision you felt unqualified for.
   What did you do? *(past behavior)*
3. When something new hit you — a term sheet, an equity question, a legal thing —
   how did you figure out what to do? *(process, evidence)*
4. What have you already tried to get on top of all of it? Courses, advisors,
   docs, tools, people? Did you pay for any of it? *(prior spend = signal)*
5. What's slipped or gone wrong because you were spread too thin? *(cost)*
6. If you could have had one thing figured out for you in month one, what would it
   have been? *(let them prioritize — still about their experience)*

Notably **absent**: "Would you use a curriculum that…?" — a leading, hypothetical,
compliment-fishing question. We test the *problem* (overwhelm, no map, ad-hoc
figuring-out), not the solution.
