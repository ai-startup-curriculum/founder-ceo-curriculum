# Founder / CEO — Job Requirements (2026-11)

> **Role level:** 20 · **Repo:** `founder-ceo-curriculum` · **Sample size:** 35 postings
> observed 2026-11-02, prefer-window last 90 days (2026-08-04 → 2026-11-02).

## Why this file exists

The Founder / CEO role is not usually posted by a traditional employer. The
sample is drawn from three surrogate channels where the requirement bar for a
founding CEO is stated explicitly:

1. **Venture-studio and residency listings** — Antler, FutureSight, A Foundery,
   AI Fund, LG NOVA (new this cycle), Deep Science Ventures, Sisu. These hire
   a founder into a studio-incubated company.
2. **VC-fronted stealth CEO searches** — Unusual Ventures, Montauk Capital
   (Outpost, AI Economics OS).
3. **Wellfound / LinkedIn / startup.jobs Co-Founder & CEO listings** where a
   technical founder is looking for a business co-founder to take the CEO seat
   (Monadical, Voicera.io, VIKR Technologies, LancerPower, One Stop Swap).

Equivalent titles counted per the packet: `Founder`, `Startup CEO`,
`Founding CEO`, `Solo Founder`, `Co-Founder & CEO`, `Founder & CEO`.

## Bottom line

**No new modules, exercises, or projects proposed this cycle.** Every
recurring requirement (posting frequency ≥ 0.30) is already covered by one of
the six existing modules, or belongs to a lower-level sibling role and is
linked out below per the ownership rule. The requirement distribution has not
materially shifted since 2026-10 — if anything, one of the two deferred items
(AI regulatory compliance) has *weakened* this cycle.

## What changed vs. 2026-10

Short answer: not enough to warrant a curriculum change.

- **Freshened sample.** 26 postings carried from the 2026-10 cycle and
  re-observed inside the 2026-11 prefer-window; 5 new postings added
  (LG NOVA × 2, Deep Science Ventures CEO Co-Founder in Residence, VIKR
  Technologies, LancerPower AI Infrastructure). Requirement frequencies moved
  by at most ±0.04. No requirement crossed the 0.30 threshold that wasn't
  already there.
- **EU AI Act compliance signal WEAKENED (not strengthened) this cycle.** The
  **EU Digital Omnibus on AI** (effective 2026-07-27) deferred stand-alone
  high-risk AI compliance from 2026-08-02 to **2027-12-02**, and
  product-embedded AI to **2028-08-02**. Transparency rules, prohibited-practice
  bans, and general-purpose AI (GPAI) duties stay in force, but the enforcement
  cliff that drove 2026-10's commentary intensification has moved more than a
  year out. 0 of 35 founder/CEO postings cite the Act. The Axipro 2026 study
  finding (>70% of governance JDs omit the Act) still holds — the signal has
  not crystallized and now has less urgency to do so.
- **AI-native operating model** commentary continues to intensify
  (Microsoft Work Trend Index 2026 *Agents, human agency, and the opportunity*;
  Forbes/CNBC on CEOs bringing AI agents to work; FI.co *The One-Person
  Unicorn*; LinkedIn Economic Graph placing *Founder* in the top-10
  fastest-growing roles across US/UK/DE/FR/IN during 2026 Q3). But the new
  LG NOVA postings — the strongest new signal this cycle — explicitly frame
  AI as the **product stack** ("model training, inference, agent
  infrastructure, orchestration, evaluation, observability"), **not** as the
  operating model. 7 of 35 postings reference AI-agent/LLM product fluency
  (frequency 0.20); 0 of 35 explicitly require *operating* the company with
  AI agents. Still below 0.30.

## Detailed evidence

### req-001 — Customer discovery & problem validation

**Covered by:** [mod-001-customer-discovery](./lessons/mod-001-customer-discovery)

22 of 35 postings. The new LG NOVA Core AI Ventures FIR is the sharpest
addition — it demands *"hard evidence of demand (paid pilots or signed
commitments)"* within a 90-day validation sprint. That phrasing is already
consistent with mod-001's discovery → design-partner → paid-pilot framing.

Selected quotes:

- FutureSight EIR — *"Engage in early-stage testing, validation, and refinement of business ideas"*
- A Foundery (shared criteria across 8 portfolio companies) — *"Reach paying customers quickly"*
- Sisu — *"Partner with fellow founders to develop a startup concept during its inception phase"*
- Montauk Capital Outpost — *"Build relationships with industrial manufacturers, logistics operators, and defense organizations to secure design partners and revenue"*
- **New: LG NOVA — *"Validate venture thesis via buyer discovery, pain-point validation, and hard evidence of demand (paid pilots or signed commitments)"***

### req-002 — Lean business modeling & unit economics

**Covered by:** [mod-002-lean-business-modeling](./lessons/mod-002-lean-business-modeling)

16 of 35 postings. A Foundery remains the most explicit, with every portfolio
company sharing *"build cash-positive operations (not valuation-focused)"* as
success criteria. Montauk AI Economics OS requires *"drive business
fundamentals including unit economics and customer retention."* New
Wellfound postings (VIKR, LancerPower) call for commercial leadership without
specifying unit-economics vocabulary.

### req-003 — Runway, operating plan, P&L ownership

**Covered by:** [mod-003-runway-and-financial-modeling](./lessons/mod-003-runway-and-financial-modeling)

14 of 35 postings. FutureSight continues to require *"own the P/L of the new
entity."* LG NOVA's residency explicitly compensates founders at $180k–$200k
during the two sprints and then transitions to seed-stage P&L ownership upon
spin-out.

### req-004 — Fundraising: pre-seed to seed

**Covered by:** [mod-004-fundraising-preseed-to-seed](./lessons/mod-004-fundraising-preseed-to-seed)

The single most-cited requirement — 26 of 35 postings (0.74, up slightly from
0.73). Every one of the 5 new postings this cycle names fundraising
explicitly. LG NOVA, Deep Science Ventures, VIKR, LancerPower all cite seed
fundraising ownership; Unusual Ventures continues to call for *"venture
fundraising, investor relations, and board management."*

Deeper post-Series-A fundraising sits at level 40
(`startup-finance-fundraising-curriculum`); Founder/CEO cites out for that
depth rather than duplicating.

### req-005 — Cap tables, SAFEs, equity

**Covered by:** [mod-005-equity-safes-cap-tables](./lessons/mod-005-equity-safes-cap-tables)

13 of 35 postings. Equity ranges remain the primary surface: Antler
*"$200k for 8% and $300k in follow-on capital"*, Voicera *"8.0%–16.0%"*,
LancerPower *"4.0%–12.0%"*, One Stop Swap *"3.0%–6.0%"*, Deep Science
Ventures *"founding team collectively holds majority equity"*. mod-005
already covers the structural work (option pool math, SAFE caps, priced-seed
dilution walk).

### req-006 — Founder-led sales & early enterprise customers

**Covered by:** [mod-006-founder-led-sales](./lessons/mod-006-founder-led-sales)

20 of 35 postings. Unusual Ventures explicitly calls for *"six- and
seven-figure ACVs, multi-stakeholder deal cycles."* Montauk Capital Outpost
requires *"secure design partners and revenue."* LG NOVA's Sprint 2
transitions the FIR to *"commercialization with early customers."* VIKR and
LancerPower both call for commercial leadership and early sales ownership.

Post-founder-led scaling (SDR playbooks, quota carriers, formal sales org)
belongs to level 30 (`startup-product-gtm-curriculum`). Founder/CEO covers the
founder-does-the-selling era only.

### req-007 — Founding team formation & first hires  <a id="req-007"></a>

**Owner:** `startup-operations-governance-curriculum` (level 50) — linked
out. Technical hiring (founding engineer, AI-engineer comp bands) is owned at
level 25 (`cto-curriculum`).

13 of 35 postings mention team-building responsibility, e.g.:

- Unusual Ventures — *"Build, lead, and mentor executive leadership team (CTO, VP Product, VP Sales, VP Engineering)"*
- FutureSight — *"Build team, product, revenue model, business, and investor base"*
- Montauk Capital Outpost — *"Recruit founding team members and establish organizational culture"*
- Sisu — *"Leadership ability to build teams from scratch"*
- Deep Science Ventures — *"Founding team collectively holds majority equity"*
- LancerPower — *"Build and lead the founding team"*

Per the ownership rule, first-hires / comp / founding-team formation belongs
to the People pillar owned at L50. The Founder/CEO curriculum links out. No
new module is proposed here.

### req-009 — Domain expertise / founder-market fit

Selection criterion, not a teachable module in isolation. Every studio and
VC-fronted listing requires relevant domain expertise and/or industry
advantage. New this cycle: LG NOVA explicitly demands *"deep expertise and
credibility in a specific industry or problem space"* **and** *"active network
of buyers and decision-makers in the domain."*

The existing curriculum exercises whatever domain expertise the learner brings
(mod-001 discovery in their own market; mod-006 sales script in their own
vertical). Nothing to add.

### req-010 — AI-native operating model (agents as workforce)

**Below threshold (0.20).** Industry commentary intensified further through
2026 Q4 (Microsoft Work Trend Index 2026 *Agents, human agency, and the
opportunity*; LinkedIn Economic Graph placing *Founder* in the top-10
fastest-growing roles across US/UK/DE/FR/IN; Forbes *The 20 New Agentic AI
Jobs*; FI.co *The One-Person Unicorn*). But posting-side evidence remains
thin: 7 of 35 postings reference *"familiarity with AI agents/LLMs"* — which
is product/vertical fluency rather than operating-model responsibility.

The new LG NOVA listings are instructive. LG NOVA's "AI Native Ventures" and
"Core AI Ventures" FIR tracks are named "AI-native" but mean *AI as the
product the venture builds* — the required experience list reads *"model
training, inference, serving, optimization, AI runtimes, agent
infrastructure, orchestration, evaluation, observability, governance, data
infrastructure."* Direct phrasing like *"operate the company with AI agents"*
or *"replace functions with agent scaffolds"* appears in zero of 35 postings.

Deferred; monitor next cycle.

### req-011 — AI regulatory compliance (EU AI Act, TX RAIGA, CA AB 2013)

**Zero of 35 postings cite it explicitly, and the enforcement signal moved
further out this cycle.** The **EU Digital Omnibus on AI** (effective
2026-07-27) deferred stand-alone high-risk AI compliance from the previously
cited 2026-08-02 deadline to **2027-12-02**, and product-embedded AI to
**2028-08-02**. Transparency rules, prohibited-practice bans, and GPAI duties
remain in force now. Enterprise procurement teams will still ask for AI Act
documentation during sales cycles, but the regulatory enforcement cliff that
drove last cycle's commentary intensification has moved more than a year out.

An Axipro 2026 governance-hiring study continues to hold at *"more than 7 in
10 governance job descriptions do not mention the EU AI Act"* — and now even
dedicated governance roles have less urgency to specify it. For founder/CEO
postings, 0 of 35. Ownership stays at L50. Deferred.
