# Founder / CEO — Job Requirements (2026-10)

> **Role level:** 20 · **Repo:** `founder-ceo-curriculum` · **Sample size:** 30 postings
> observed 2026-10-02, prefer-window last 90 days (2026-07-02 → 2026-10-02).

## Why this file exists

The Founder / CEO role is not usually posted by a traditional employer. The
sample is drawn from three surrogate channels where the requirement bar for a
founding CEO is stated explicitly:

1. **Venture-studio and residency listings** — Antler, FutureSight, A Foundery,
   AI Fund, Sisu, Spring Innovation Fund, Deep Science Ventures, Cache
   Ventures, Platform Venture Studio. These hire a founder into a
   studio-incubated company.
2. **VC-fronted stealth CEO searches** — Unusual Ventures, Montauk Capital
   (Outpost, AI Economics OS), 16VC EIR, Banyan.
3. **Wellfound / LinkedIn / startup.jobs Co-Founder & CEO listings** where a
   technical founder is looking for a business co-founder to take the CEO seat.

Equivalent titles counted per the packet: `Founder`, `Startup CEO`,
`Founding CEO`, `Solo Founder`, `Co-Founder & CEO`, `Founder & CEO`.

## Bottom line

**No new modules, exercises, or projects proposed this cycle.** Every
recurring requirement (posting frequency ≥ 0.30) is already covered by one of
the six existing modules, or belongs to a lower-level sibling role and is
linked out below per the ownership rule. The requirement distribution has not
materially shifted since the 2026-09 cycle one month ago.

## What changed vs. 2026-09

Short answer: not enough to warrant a curriculum change.

- **Freshened sample.** 10 postings that were observed in 2026-09 remain live
  and have been re-observed within the 90-day window; 12 new postings have
  been added (notably Montauk Capital Outpost, AI Fund Strata, VistIQ.ai,
  LUMARA Collective, Healthcare Shares, Sisu, Spring Innovation Fund, Cache
  Ventures, Deep Science Ventures). Requirement frequencies moved by at most
  ±0.03; no requirement crossed the 0.30 threshold that wasn't already there.
- **EU AI Act August 2, 2026 enforcement deadline** has now passed. Industry
  commentary (HK Law, Nasdaq Governance Insights, AI Conference London)
  elevates it to a board-level fiduciary duty, but a 2026 governance-hiring
  study found "more than 7 in 10 governance job descriptions do not mention
  the EU AI Act." In the 30-posting founder/CEO sample, zero postings cite it
  explicitly. Still below 0.30.
- **AI-native operating model** has intensified in commentary (TechCrunch
  Disrupt 2026 "Hiring When AI Is a Co-Founder" panel, Sept 16, 2026;
  Anthropic's Dario Amodei prediction of a billion-dollar one-person company
  within 2026; McKinsey's *Seven Operating Truths of AI-Native Companies*; FI.co
  *Your First Ten Hires Are AI Agents*). Posting language has crept up only
  slightly — six of 30 postings now reference AI-agent product fluency, but
  that is **vertical/product familiarity**, not **operating-model
  responsibility**. Frequency ~0.20. Still below threshold.

## Detailed evidence

### req-001 — Customer discovery & problem validation

**Covered by:** [mod-001-customer-discovery](./lessons/mod-001-customer-discovery)

19 of 30 postings. Studio listings (FutureSight, A Foundery, Sisu, Spring
Innovation Fund) require the incoming CEO to run early-stage testing,
validation, and insight synthesis before shipping product. Wellfound and
Montauk/VistIQ listings require it via "customer development," "secure design
partners," and "define the ideal customer profile" language.

Selected quotes:

- FutureSight EIR — *"Engage in early-stage testing, validation, and refinement of business ideas"*
- A Foundery (shared criteria across 8 portfolio companies) — *"Reach paying customers quickly"*
- Sisu — *"Partner with fellow founders to develop a startup concept during its inception phase"*
- Montauk Capital — Outpost — *"Build relationships with industrial manufacturers, logistics operators, and defense organizations to secure design partners and revenue"*

### req-002 — Lean business modeling & unit economics

**Covered by:** [mod-002-lean-business-modeling](./lessons/mod-002-lean-business-modeling)

14 of 30 postings. A Foundery remains the most explicit, with every portfolio
company (PodForge, LedgerOps.ai, CollectIQ, PropDesk, ResolveAI, WebRegistry,
PowerOpsHQ, Everonn.ai) sharing *"build cash-positive operations (not
valuation-focused)"* as success criteria. Montauk AI Economics OS requires
*"drive business fundamentals including unit economics and customer
retention."*

### req-003 — Runway, operating plan, P&L ownership

**Covered by:** [mod-003-runway-and-financial-modeling](./lessons/mod-003-runway-and-financial-modeling)

12 of 30 postings. FutureSight continues to require *"own the P/L of the new
entity."* Qubic Digital Services (Fractional CEO) and carebeam (Healthcare
Startup CEO) both require explicit financial-plan ownership.

### req-004 — Fundraising: pre-seed to seed

**Covered by:** [mod-004-fundraising-preseed-to-seed](./lessons/mod-004-fundraising-preseed-to-seed)

The single most-cited requirement — 22 of 30 postings (0.73). Explicitly named
in Antler residency ($500k pre-seed pitch), FutureSight ("prepare for pre-seed
and seed fundraising"), Unusual Ventures ("venture fundraising, investor
relations, board management"), Montauk Capital Outpost ("develop narratives
and lead seed financing rounds"), Sisu ("pursue capital through fundraising"),
AI Fund, and VistIQ.ai.

Deeper post-Series-A fundraising sits at level 40
(`startup-finance-fundraising-curriculum`); Founder/CEO cites out for that
depth rather than duplicating.

### req-005 — Cap tables, SAFEs, equity

**Covered by:** [mod-005-equity-safes-cap-tables](./lessons/mod-005-equity-safes-cap-tables)

10 of 30 postings, mostly implied through equity-structure lines ("$200k for
8% and $300k in follow-on capital" — Antler; "equity: 8.0%–16.0%" — Voicera;
"3.0%–6.0%" — One Stop Swap; "1.0%–12.0%" — Silver Foxx; "equity: 20.0%–33.0%"
— Pitch1000; "meaningful founder equity" — FutureSight portfolio).

### req-006 — Founder-led sales & early enterprise customers

**Covered by:** [mod-006-founder-led-sales](./lessons/mod-006-founder-led-sales)

17 of 30 postings. Unusual Ventures explicitly calls for *"six- and
seven-figure ACVs, multi-stakeholder deal cycles."* Montauk Capital Outpost
requires *"secure design partners and revenue."* Montauk AI Economics OS
requires *"product-market fit and repeatable sales process track record."*
FutureSight requires *"create prototypes to validate and sell potential
solutions."*

Post-founder-led scaling (SDR playbooks, quota carriers, formal sales org)
belongs to level 30 (`startup-product-gtm-curriculum`). Founder/CEO covers the
founder-does-the-selling era only.

### req-007 — Founding team formation & first hires  <a id="req-007"></a>

**Owner:** `startup-operations-governance-curriculum` (level 50) — linked
out. Technical hiring (founding engineer, AI-engineer comp bands) is owned at
level 25 (`cto-curriculum`).

12 of 30 postings mention team-building responsibility, e.g.:

- Unusual Ventures — *"Build, lead, and mentor executive leadership team (CTO, VP Product, VP Sales, VP Engineering)"*
- FutureSight — *"Build team, product, revenue model, business, and investor base"*
- Montauk Capital Outpost — *"Recruit founding team members and establish organizational culture"*
- Sisu — *"Leadership ability to build teams from scratch"*

Per the ownership rule, first-hires / comp / founding-team formation belongs
to the People pillar owned at L50. The Founder/CEO curriculum links out. No
new module is proposed here.

### req-009 — Domain expertise / founder-market fit

Selection criterion, not a teachable module in isolation. Every studio and
VC-fronted listing requires relevant domain expertise and/or industry
advantage. The FutureSight portfolio (HawkAI logistics, Omnex e-commerce,
Concordance healthcare, Maestro retail, Weli AI wealthtech, Reclaim healthcare
revenue, Crossfoot accounting, InfluTracker content, Foundry recruiting,
LucentAI legal, Closeline mortgage) each specifies vertical-specific domain
knowledge. Montauk Outpost requires *"deep industrial customer network
(manufacturing, warehousing, logistics, energy)."*

The existing curriculum exercises whatever domain expertise the learner brings
(mod-001 discovery in their own market; mod-006 sales script in their own
vertical). Nothing to add.

### req-010 — AI-native operating model (agents as workforce)

**Below threshold (0.20).** Industry commentary intensified sharply in 2026
Q3 — TechCrunch Disrupt 2026 ran a panel titled *"Hiring When AI Is a
Co-Founder"* on Sept 16, 2026; Dario Amodei (Anthropic) attached a 70–80%
probability to a billion-dollar one-person company arriving within 2026;
McKinsey published *The Seven Operating Truths of AI-Native Companies* in 2026
Q3. But posting-side evidence remains thin: 6 of 30 postings reference
"familiarity with AI agents/LLMs," which is product/vertical fluency rather
than operating-model responsibility. Direct phrasing like *"operate the
company with AI agents"* or *"replace functions with agent scaffolds"* appears
in zero of 30 postings. Deferred; monitor next cycle.

### req-011 — AI regulatory compliance (EU AI Act, TX RAIGA, CA AB 2013)

**Zero of 30 postings cite it explicitly.** The EU AI Act's August 2, 2026
enforcement deadline has now passed, and board-level fiduciary-duty framing
has intensified in commentary (Nasdaq Governance Insights, HK Law, AI
Conference London, DPO Europe). But an Axipro 2026 governance-hiring gap study
found *"more than 7 in 10 governance job descriptions do not mention the EU AI
Act"* — i.e., even dedicated governance roles underspecify it. For founder/CEO
postings specifically, the signal is still absent from the hiring language.
Ownership belongs at L50. Deferred.
