# Founder / CEO — Job Requirements (2026-09)

> **Role level:** 20 · **Repo:** `founder-ceo-curriculum` · **Sample size:** 30 postings
> observed 2026-09-02, prefer-window last 90 days.

## Why this file exists

The Founder / CEO role is not usually posted by a traditional employer. The
sample was drawn from three surrogate channels where the requirement bar for a
founding CEO is stated explicitly:

1. **Venture-studio and residency listings** — Antler, FutureSight, A Foundery,
   AI Fund. These hire a founder into a studio-incubated company.
2. **VC-fronted stealth CEO searches** — Unusual Ventures, Banyan, EIR programs.
3. **Wellfound / LinkedIn / startup.jobs Co-Founder & CEO listings** where a
   technical founder is looking for a business co-founder to take the CEO seat.

Equivalent titles counted per the packet: `Founder`, `Startup CEO`,
`Founding CEO`, `Solo Founder`, `Co-Founder & CEO`, `Founder & CEO`.

## Bottom line

**No new modules, exercises, or projects proposed this cycle.** Every
recurring requirement (posting frequency ≥ 0.30) is already covered by one of
the six existing modules, or belongs to a lower-level sibling role and is
linked out below per the ownership rule.

## Detailed evidence

### req-001 — Customer discovery & problem validation

**Covered by:** [mod-001-customer-discovery](./lessons/mod-001-customer-discovery)

Explicit in 17 of 30 postings. Studio listings (FutureSight, A Foundery, AI
Fund) require the incoming CEO to run early-stage testing, validation, and
insight synthesis before shipping product. Wellfound co-founder listings
implicitly require it via "founder-led sales / early customer engagement"
language.

Selected quotes:

- FutureSight EIR — *"Engage in early-stage testing, validation, and refinement of business ideas"*
- A Foundery — *"Clear path to paying customers"*
- 16VC EIR — *"Demonstrated success in B2B AI or B2B SaaS go-to-market, including sales and customer engagement"*

The 2026 shift toward AI-moderated customer interviews (compressing the
discovery cycle from 3 weeks to ~3 days, per Perspective AI's YC W26 study) can
be woven into the existing exercise brief incrementally — no new module required.

### req-002 — Lean business modeling & unit economics

**Covered by:** [mod-002-lean-business-modeling](./lessons/mod-002-lean-business-modeling)

14 of 30 postings require it, most explicitly A Foundery's cash-positive
unit-economics-within-90-days framing and Unusual Ventures' "strong business
acumen across product, technology, and go-to-market functions."

### req-003 — Runway, operating plan, P&L ownership

**Covered by:** [mod-003-runway-and-financial-modeling](./lessons/mod-003-runway-and-financial-modeling)

12 of 30 postings. FutureSight explicitly requires "own the P&L of the new
entity." Studio listings additionally require preparing an operating plan
suitable for a pre-seed pitch.

### req-004 — Fundraising: pre-seed to seed

**Covered by:** [mod-004-fundraising-preseed-to-seed](./lessons/mod-004-fundraising-preseed-to-seed)

The single most-cited requirement — 21 of 30 postings (0.73). Explicitly named
in Antler ($500k investment committee pitch), FutureSight ("prepare for
pre-seed and seed fundraising"), Unusual Ventures ("venture fundraising,
investor relations, board management"), and every "Co-Founder — Fundraising &
Capital" Wellfound listing.

Deeper post-Series-A fundraising sits at level 40
(`startup-finance-fundraising-curriculum`); Founder/CEO cites out for that
depth rather than duplicating.

### req-005 — Cap tables, SAFEs, equity

**Covered by:** [mod-005-equity-safes-cap-tables](./lessons/mod-005-equity-safes-cap-tables)

10 of 30 postings, mostly implied through equity-structure lines
("$200k for 8% and $300k in follow-on capital" — Antler; "Founder equity 8–16%" —
Voicera; "Co-founder partnership with meaningful founder equity" — FutureSight).

### req-006 — Founder-led sales & early enterprise customers

**Covered by:** [mod-006-founder-led-sales](./lessons/mod-006-founder-led-sales)

16 of 30 postings. Unusual Ventures explicitly calls for enterprise ACVs in
the six-to-seven figures; FutureSight requires "create prototypes to validate
and sell potential solutions ... launch initial product and achieve market
traction."

Post-founder-led scaling (SDR playbooks, quota carriers, formal sales org)
belongs to level 30 (`startup-product-gtm-curriculum`). Founder/CEO covers the
founder-does-the-selling era only.

### req-007 — Founding team formation & first hires  <a id="req-007"></a>

**Owner:** `startup-operations-governance-curriculum` (level 50) — linked
out. Technical hiring (founding engineer, AI-engineer comp bands) is owned
at level 25 (`cto-curriculum`).

12 of 30 postings mention team-building responsibility, e.g.:

- Unusual Ventures — *"Build, lead, and mentor executive leadership team (CTO, VP Product, VP Sales, VP Engineering)"*
- FutureSight — *"Build team, product, revenue model, business, and investor base"*

Per the ownership rule ("assign the primary coverage to the lowest-level role
where it is genuinely required"), first-hires / comp / founding-team formation
belongs to the People pillar owned at L50. The Founder/CEO curriculum links
out. No new module is proposed here.

lands:

### req-008 — Board management & investor governance  <a id="req-008"></a>

**Owner:** `startup-operations-governance-curriculum` (level 50) — linked out.

Frequency ~0.20, below the ≥0.30 threshold. Unusual Ventures is the most
explicit posting on it; some Wellfound fundraising-lead co-founder listings
imply it. Ownership sits at L50 (governance/fiduciary duty). No new module
proposed.

External resources:

- [Carta — Board of Directors: What Founders Need to Know](https://carta.com/learn/startups/private-companies/board-of-directors/)
- [Rho — Build an Effective Startup Board](https://www.rho.co/blog/startup-board-composition)

### req-009 — Domain expertise / founder-market fit

Selection criterion, not a teachable module in isolation. Every studio listing
requires "relevant domain expertise and/or industry advantage." The existing
curriculum exercises whatever domain expertise the learner brings (mod-001
discovery on their own market, mod-006 sales script in their own vertical).
Nothing to add.

### req-010 — AI-native operating model (agents as workforce)

**Below threshold (0.20).** Fortune, Forbes, and Foundra commentary in 2026
describes solo founders using AI agents as their "first ten hires" and hitting
a ceiling at pre-seed enterprise sales. Direct posting evidence remains thin
(3 of 30), so this is deferred. Monitor next cycle.

### req-011 — AI regulatory compliance (EU AI Act, TX RAIGA, CA AB 2013)

**Below threshold (0.10).** More discussed in industry commentary than in
postings. Ownership belongs at L50. Deferred.

External resource:

- [AttorneyX — AI Compliance for Startups: What Founders Need to Do in 2026](https://attorneyx.law/blog/ai-compliance-for-startups-what-founders-need-to-do-in-2026/)

- 30 postings analyzed (target ≥25).
